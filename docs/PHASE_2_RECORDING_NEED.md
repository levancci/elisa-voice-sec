# Additional recording requirement before ranking

No ranking decision is made from the current results. Each split currently
has only two distinct speakers and 40 genuine/40 impostor trial pairs.

Recommended next collection: **7 additional participants**, bringing the
study to 12 speakers total. Use six speakers for tuning and six held out for
evaluation, with no speaker appearing in both sets.

For every new participant, follow `docs/RECORDING_PROTOCOL.md` exactly:

- 5 enrollment strings covering all ten digits, in quiet and noisy conditions;
- 10 fresh four-digit verification strings in quiet;
- 10 different verification strings in noise;
- 4-second, 16 kHz, mono, 16-bit PCM WAV;
- pseudonymous participant IDs and written consent;
- device/microphone and noise condition recorded;
- tuning and held-out recordings kept separate.

Cross-speaker comparisons generate impostor trials. Add wrong-digit, replay,
and splicing recordings only in the later attack/evaluation set, not for
threshold tuning.
